## Purpose

This Architecture Vision elicits the significant architecture drivers such as business, functional, nonfunctional requirements and constraints, defines architecture, and develops a roadmap for the eDiscovery and Litigation Document Review Platform implementation. The document is intended as a primary technical guidance into solution implementation for the development team.

The document answers the Client's request for proposal, listed in [References](#references). The Client is an international law firm with 1,400 lawyers in 14 offices, whose litigation, arbitration and regulatory-investigation practices process roughly 220 million documents per year across 300 active matters. Document review is outsourced today to three external hosting vendors at a cost of EUR 18.5 million per year, and the Client intends to replace them with a firm-operated eDiscovery platform hosted under its own security controls.

The solution architecture is designed following the guiding principles outlined in Appendix A — Architecture Design Methodology.
