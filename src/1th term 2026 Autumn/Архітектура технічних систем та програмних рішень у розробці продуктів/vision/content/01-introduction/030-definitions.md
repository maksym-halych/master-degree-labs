## Definitions

The Definitions section lists the acronyms and terms used in this document which might possess lesser familiarity or double meaning to the reader.

+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| \#                 | Term                                                  | Definition                                                                                                                                                                   |
+====================+=======================================================+==============================================================================================================================================================================+
| 1\.                | Continuous active learning                            | A machine-learning review method that retrains the model on every reviewer decision and keeps the likely relevant documents first in the queue.                              |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2\.                | Early case assessment                                 | Analysis of collected data before review to estimate its volume, cost and key facts and to cull irrelevant data.                                                             |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3\.                | eDiscovery                                            | Electronic discovery: the identification, preservation, collection, processing, review and production of electronically stored information for litigation or investigations. |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4\.                | Legal hold                                            | A notice that instructs the persons who hold data relevant to a matter to preserve it, and suspends its routine deletion.                                                    |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5\.                | Matter                                                | A single litigation, arbitration or regulatory investigation the firm handles for a client; the unit of data, access and billing.                                            |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6\.                | Privilege                                             | Legal professional privilege: protection that allows a document, such as lawyer-client advice, to be withheld from production.                                               |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 7\.                | Production                                            | Delivery of relevant, non-privileged documents to the opposing party or the court in an agreed format; distinct from the production environment the platform runs in.        |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 8\.                | Redaction                                             | Masking privileged or sensitive content in a document before production.                                                                                                     |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
