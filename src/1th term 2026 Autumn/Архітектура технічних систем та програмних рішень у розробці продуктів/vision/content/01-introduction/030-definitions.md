## Definitions

The Definitions section lists the acronyms and terms used in this document which might possess lesser familiarity or double meaning to the reader.

+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| \#                 | Term                                                  | Definition                                                                                                                                                                     |
+====================+=======================================================+================================================================================================================================================================================+
| 1\.                | Continuous active learning                            | A machine-learning review method that retrains the model on every reviewer decision and keeps the likely relevant documents first in the queue.                                |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2\.                | Custodian                                             | A person who keeps data that is related to a matter, for example an employee with a related mailbox.                                                                           |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3\.                | Early case assessment                                 | Analysis of collected data before review to estimate its volume, cost and key facts and to cull irrelevant data.                                                               |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4\.                | eDiscovery                                            | Electronic discovery: the identification, preservation, collection, processing, review and production of electronically stored information for litigation or investigations.   |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 5\.                | Ethical wall                                          | An access barrier. It keeps persons with a conflict of interest away from the data of a matter.                                                                                |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 6\.                | Go-live                                               | The first production release of the platform with real client matters. Some objectives start their time frame at go-live.                                                      |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 7\.                | Legal hold                                            | A notice that instructs the persons who hold data relevant to a matter to preserve it, and suspends its routine deletion.                                                      |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 8\.                | Matter                                                | A single litigation, arbitration or regulatory investigation the firm handles for a client; the unit of data, access and billing.                                              |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 9\.                | Precision                                             | The percentage of the documents that the model marks as relevant and that are really relevant.                                                                                 |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 10\.               | Privilege                                             | Legal professional privilege: protection that allows a document, such as lawyer-client advice, to be withheld from production.                                                 |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 11\.               | Production                                            | Delivery of relevant, non-privileged documents to the opposing party or the court in an agreed format; distinct from the production environment the platform runs in.          |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 12\.               | Recall                                                | The percentage of all relevant documents in a matter that the model finds.                                                                                                     |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 13\.               | Redaction                                             | Masking privileged or sensitive content in a document before production.                                                                                                       |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 14\.               | TAR                                                   | Technology-assisted review: a review method in which a machine-learning model learns from reviewer decisions and sorts documents by relevance.                                 |
+--------------------+-------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
