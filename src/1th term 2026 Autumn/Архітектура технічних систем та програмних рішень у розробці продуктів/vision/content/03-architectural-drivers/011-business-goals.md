### Business Goals

The section enumerates essential business goals for the solution.

+--------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| \#           | Description                                                                                          |
+==============+======================================================================================================+
| BG-1         | []{#goal-bg-1}Decrease the document review cost and keep the review margin in the firm               |
+--------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BG-2         | []{#goal-bg-2}Offer fixed-fee litigation services, to keep and win panel appointments                |
+--------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BG-3         | []{#goal-bg-3}Make the review process defensible in court, from legal hold to production             |
+--------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BG-4         | []{#goal-bg-4}Keep client data secure and in the jurisdictions that the clients approve              |
+--------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| BG-5         | []{#goal-bg-5}Stop the dependence on external hosting vendors                                        |
+--------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
